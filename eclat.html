Our Story

A journey of passion, innovation, and culinary excellence

2010 - Humble Beginnings

Chef Alexandre Dubois opened Éclat as a small 30-seat bistro with a simple mission: to showcase seasonal ingredients through French technique.

2012 - First Recognition

Received our first Michelin star, cementing Éclat's place in the city's culinary scene and allowing for expansion of both kitchen and dining room.

2015 - Relocation & Growth

Moved to our current location in the historic district, featuring an open kitchen, private dining room, and expanded wine cellar.

2018 - Second Star

Awarded a second Michelin star for our innovative tasting menus that blend French technique with global influences.

Present - Continuing Excellence

Today, Éclat continues to push boundaries while maintaining our commitment to quality, seasonality, and memorable dining experiences.

Chef Alexandre Dubois

Born in Lyon, France, Chef Dubois trained under some of Europe's most celebrated chefs before bringing his vision to North America.

His philosophy centers on respecting ingredients while innovating with technique, creating dishes that are both familiar and surprising.
